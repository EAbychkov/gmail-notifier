Every Gmail inbox you’re signed in to, in one panel.

Mail Deck sits on the Chrome toolbar. Read whole conversations, search every inbox, archive, delete or put mail off till later, across all your Google accounts, without opening a Gmail tab. And it is quick at all of it.

Fast, all the way through

  • Every inbox at once

    All your accounts are checked side by side, not one after another, so the count is fresh as soon as the slowest inbox answers. A Gmail that hangs no longer holds up the rest.

  • Messages open on the click

    Point at a message and it is fetched before you click. The last ones you read stay ready for the rest of the session.

  • The panel opens ready

    Stars and the mail past the first twenty are remembered for a few minutes, so opening the panel again asks Gmail nothing.

  • Actions never get lost

    Archive and close the panel straight away: the action still reaches Gmail once its five-second Undo is over, and the count drops at once.

A closer look

What it does

  • Each account at a glance

    Every Google account signed in to the browser shows with its own picture, a colour and an unread count. Pick a chip to see one inbox only.

  • Read in place

    The whole conversation opens right in the panel, attachments included. Gmail opens only when you ask it to.

  • Undo for five seconds

    Archived or deleted the wrong one? Press Undo in the notice, or Z.

  • Reply and star

    Reply opens Gmail’s compose window, already addressed. Star a message without leaving the panel.

  • Labels, not just the inbox

    Choose which Gmail labels to watch, separately for each account, or only the Primary tab.

  • Search every inbox

    One search runs in all your Google accounts at once.

  • Remind me later

    Put a message away until this evening, tomorrow or next week. It comes back with a notification.

  • Your order, your side panel

    Drag the inboxes into your order, keep the deck open beside the page, open it with Alt+Shift+M.

  • Notifications and sound

    A desktop notice and an optional sound for new mail, with quiet hours, quiet inboxes and rules for senders.

One key per action

  • RMark as read
  • EArchive
  • DelDelete
  • !Report spam
  • SStar
  • ZUndo
  • BRemind me later
  • /Search
  • OOpen in Gmail
  • JNext message

The keys go by position, so they work with any keyboard layout.

Nothing in between

Mail Deck has no servers and no analytics. It talks to Google only, with the session your browser already has, and never sees your password.
